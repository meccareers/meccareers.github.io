Internship Opportunity: Summer 2026 Cybersecurity Intern @ New York Power Authority (NYPA)

📍 Location: White Plains, NY
📅 Paid 10-week Summer Developmental Internship: June 8 – August 13, 2026
💲 Pay: $18.00 – $25.00 per hour

At the New York Power Authority, you will be empowered to think big, do good, and transform the energy industry. We are a collaborative team of over 2,400 energy technologists, IT specialists, business experts, hydro engineers, and diverse professionals leading the energy revolution. With state-of-the-art technology, advanced research and development, a modernized infrastructure, and a focus on environmental, social and governance (ESG), we provide New Yorkers with low-cost, clean, reliable power — and we are well on the way to becoming the first end-to-end digital utility in the country.

The Information Security team is responsible for protecting NYPA’s critical infrastructure, information assets, and technology environments from evolving cyber threats. As cybersecurity risks continue to increase in frequency and sophistication, additional resources are essential to support the organization’s Security Operations Center (SOC), Vulnerability Management, and Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI) functions. This internship opportunity will provide valuable support to key cybersecurity initiatives while helping develop the next generation of cybersecurity professionals.

We are seeking a highly motivated student pursuing a degree in Cybersecurity, Information Security, Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field. The Cybersecurity Intern will gain hands-on experience supporting security monitoring, threat detection, vulnerability assessments, cyber threat analysis, and security operations activities within a critical infrastructure environment.

The intern will assist the Security Operations Center with monitoring and triaging security alerts, analyzing potential cybersecurity incidents, and documenting investigative findings. The individual will also support the Vulnerability Management program by helping identify, track, prioritize, and report on vulnerabilities across enterprise systems and applications. In addition, the intern will contribute to Cyber Threat Intelligence efforts by researching emerging threats, threat actors, indicators of compromise, and industry trends relevant to the energy sector.

The successful candidate will work closely with cybersecurity professionals across multiple disciplines and will be encouraged to take initiative in identifying opportunities to improve security processes, enhance threat visibility, and support ongoing cybersecurity projects. This position offers a unique opportunity to gain exposure to cybersecurity operations within one of the nation’s largest public power organizations.

Our paid 10-week Summer Developmental Internship program runs from June 8th - August 13th, 2026. As an Intern, you will receive professional development, on-the job experience in the energy industry, exposure to executives, and networking opportunities to help build a solid foundation for your future career.

If you are passionate about our mission – to lead the transition to a carbon-free, economically vibrant New York through customer partnerships, innovative energy solutions, community engagement and the responsible supply of affordable, clean, and reliable electricity, we are looking for you!

Responsibilities:

  • Participate in department-specific projects, analysis, research methods and learn project management skills
  • Support team in developing business solutions that contribute to NYPA’s VISION2030 strategic goals
  • Research industry trends and emerging technologies to contribute to the continuous improvement of our programs
  • Other projects as assigned; will vary based on focus area

Knowledge, Skills and Abilities:

  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office programs, especially Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook
  • Excellent verbal and written communication skills
  • Strong organizational skills and ability to multi-task with competing priorities
  • Ability to work both independently and collaborate as part of a team
  • Provide positive customer service for internal and external stakeholders

Education, Experience and Certifications:

  • Enrollment in an associate’s, undergraduate or graduate program at a college or university is required. Recent graduates are not eligible for our Developmental Internship program.
  • Must be legally eligible to work in the United States for the duration of the internship from June 8th - August 13th

Compensation:

The New York Power Authority is committed to providing fair, competitive, and market-informed compensation. The hourly rate for this position is based on the level of education at the time the intern program begins:

  • Sophomore: $18.00/hour
  • Junior: $22.00/hour
  • Senior (or May 2028 graduate): $22.00/hour
  • Current graduate/law student: $25.00/hour

Application Deadline: December 31st, 2026.
