About the Center

The MEC career center provides comprehensive career development and advising support for all students of the engineering and computer majors programs. We are dedicated to helping students throughout their career journey to help them not only find a job, but develop a meaningful career in the fields of engineering and computer majors.

Our career services staff works with each student to craft an individualized career action plan, and provides helpful advice on achieving professional goals and milestones. We also provide opportunities for professional advancement through employer information sessions, seminars, company visits, career fairs, and career-related presentations.
